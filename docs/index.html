How to use the mockups

  1. Try the three layouts. The buttons along the top marked Gallery Led, Booking Led and Story Led. They are the same information in a different order, and the order changes what a visitor sees first.
  2. Then try the four colours. Any colour scheme works with any layout, so you can like the shape of one and the look of another. Say so — that combination is a five-second change for us.
  3. Press “Compare all three.” Puts the layouts side by side so the differences are obvious at a glance.
  4. Switch to “Phone.” Most people will find you from Instagram on a phone, so this view matters more than the desktop one.

Two things to expect

The photographs are missing. Every grey striped box is where one of your images goes. We did not want to fill them with stock photos of other people's families — that would flatter the design and tell you nothing. Send fifteen or twenty of your favourites and they will all fill in.

Some details are still guesses. Anything with a faint highlight behind it is something we made up because we did not know the answer. Your prices, your bio, your FAQs and your galleries are all real and correct. You can switch the highlighting off with the “Mark placeholders” tick-box if you would rather see it clean.
