“Celebrate life and make our time stand still.” 
Photography has been my passion since 2014, when I first discovered the magic of freezing a fleeting moment in time. What started as a hobby quickly became a calling — a way to help people see the beauty in their everyday lives. 
Based in Plainfield, Illinois, I specialize in family, senior, personal branding, and pet photography. Most people who write to me start with some version of I hate having my picture taken . We start there, and then we go for a walk.
